Incorporating both business acumen and computer proficiency, the Business Analytics major equips students with essential skills for leveraging technology as a strategic decision-making tool. This interdisciplinary program not only hones computer aptitude but also cultivates expertise in a business domain where these competencies are indispensable.

In today's competitive landscape, employers seek graduates who offer more than just subject-specific knowledge. They value individuals with diverse skill sets capable of contributing to various facets of their organizations. Through Business Analytics, students acquire the versatile skill set demanded by modern businesses, positioning them as valuable assets in any professional setting.

What will I study?

Students majoring in Business Analytics study several different components of business administration. The combination of core courses and specialization courses produces well-rounded graduates who can make quick, highly informed decisions. They are prepared to succeed in today's highly competitive business environment. They will be unique and valuable employees.

Career Opportunities

In addition to the traditional jobs available to graduates majoring in these areas, Business Analytics graduates may fill Decision Support positions. In these positions, they provide information to higher management in a decision support role. Graduates from this program are especially attractive to employers because of their multiple skills.
