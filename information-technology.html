The Information Systems and Technology program offers a distinctive blend of business acumen and technical prowess, providing undergraduate students with a competitive edge in today's rapidly evolving business landscape.

As a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration student specializing in Information Systems and Technology, you'll delve into traditional business domains including Finance, Marketing, Accounting, Production/Inventory Operations, and Economics. Moreover, you'll gain comprehensive expertise in computerized automation and information systems development.

ODU's Information Technology program stands out for its commitment to delivering a well-rounded education that integrates general business principles with Information Systems and Technology. You'll not only grasp the fundamentals of business operations but also master advanced technical proficiencies in relational databases, computer networking, architecture, and programming.

Our curriculum emphasizes the application of these technical skills in real-world scenarios, equipping you with the tools and techniques necessary to thrive in dynamic business environments — where a deep understanding of both automation technology and its business context is paramount.

Systems Analyst

Plays a significant role in the analysis and design of formal systems. The written and oral communication skills reinforced throughout the program ensure graduates interact effectively with both technical personnel and the business people they support.

Program Manager

In demand in today's project-oriented business world. Responsible for the planning and oversight of modern business system development. The major requires three semesters of study in the methods and techniques used to succeed in this career path.

Systems Integrator

A critical skill in a world where change is constant. Requires understanding the many factors that must be addressed during conversion when old systems are replaced with new ones.

Web Developer

The internet is a crucial component of business success. As emphasis continues to shift toward internet-based applications, demand for those on the cutting edge of web application development remains strong.

Database Administrator

Responsible for the development, operation, and maintenance of an organization's data. In addition to planning and implementing databases, they monitor database activity to ensure the integrity and availability of this shared resource.

Network Administrator

Responsible for ensuring voice, data, and image transmission networks are available and optimized for all of an organization's users. Telecommunications remains one of the most rapidly growing areas in information systems.
